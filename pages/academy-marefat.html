Marefat of the Imam of the Time

Do you know the Imam of your time?

Not his name. Him. Why he is hidden, why he is alive, and why he is waiting — for you.

Prelude

A prayer that is a map

Imam al-Sadiq (as) taught a supplication for the age of occultation. It is three lines long, and it is the whole of this journey.

You cannot recognise the Proof without first recognising the Messenger who announced him. So we begin where the light began.
