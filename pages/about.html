About

AHD-E-ZUHOOR is a guided knowledge, learning and research journey centred on Imam-e-Zamana, Imam al-Mahdi (ATFS).

It brings together an introduction to the Imam, the prophets whose lives point to him, duas and hadiths, a library of books, a learning academy for children and adults (Qur'an, beliefs, fiqh, ma'rifat and languages, taught by Zahra and Ali), and a research archive.

The site is free to use. It has no adverts and no user accounts, and it can be installed on a phone as an app.

Privacy

AHD-E-ZUHOOR has no accounts, no adverts and no analytics or tracking. This is everything the site keeps or sends.

Kept only on your device

  • Your choices: the site language, sound on or off, and whether you have seen the opening screen.
  • Your learning progress: courses chosen, episodes watched, quiz progress.
  • Copies of pages, books and audio you have already opened, so that the app works offline.

None of this leaves your device. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes it.

Ask Zahra

Your question is sent to the AHD-E-ZUHOOR server (on Cloudflare) and answered by an AI model on Cloudflare Workers AI, using passages from sistani.org.

  • The text of your question is not stored, and neither is your IP address.
  • To check the quality of answers, a technical record of each answer is kept: the language, the search words used on sistani.org, which passages were considered and cited, and how long it took.
  • To prevent misuse, questions are counted per visitor per hour using a scrambled code made from the connection, which cannot be turned back into an IP address.

Please do not type personal details (names, phone numbers, addresses) into your question.

The covenant pledge (Research Archive)

If you renew your covenant, only a count for the day is kept. You are recognised for that day only by a scrambled code, so that you are counted once; no IP address is stored.

Microphone and location

  • Qur'an "Recite": your browser's own speech recognition listens while you recite. In some browsers (such as Chrome and Edge) the browser sends the audio to its maker to be turned into words. The recordings for comparing your tajweed stay on your device and are never uploaded.
  • Qibla direction: your location is used on your device only, to work out the direction, and is not sent anywhere.

Both ask for your permission first, and you can say no.

Other services your device contacts

To show some pages, your device loads fonts from Google Fonts, and Qur'an text and audio from api.alquran.cloud, everyayah.com and Quran.com. Like any website, these services can see your IP address, under their own privacy policies.

Children

Much of the Academy is made for children. The site never asks for a child's name or any personal details. We suggest children use Ask Zahra with a parent.
