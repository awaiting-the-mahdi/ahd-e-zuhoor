Who is Imam al-Mahdi (atfs)?

The awaited hope of humanity, a sunrise of justice after the long night.

↓

Who is he?

Names & titlesMuhammad ibn al-Hasan; al-Mahdi, al-Qa’im, Sahib al-Zaman
FatherImam Hasan al-Askari (as), the 11th Imam
Birth15 Sha’ban 255 AH, Samarra
PositionThe 12th and final Imam from the Prophet’s family

The journey

255 AH · Birth

Born in Samarra to Imam al-Askari (as).

260 AH · Imamate begins

After his father’s martyrdom, he becomes the Imam at a young age.

260–329 AH · Minor Occultation

Guidance reaches the community through four special deputies.

329 AH – today · Major Occultation

Direct contact ends; the community looks to scholars and to prayer.

The promised dawn

He will emerge to establish justice and peace across the earth.
