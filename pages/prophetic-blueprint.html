The Prophetic
Blueprint

Before the final promise, there were generations of Prophets, divine missions and signs. Explore their lives and discover what authentic sources reveal about their relationship to the Imam of our time.

ONE DIVINE STORY

The Prophets were sent in different eras, to different communities, carrying the message of Allah. Their lives offer lessons in faith, patience, justice, leadership and divine promise. This journey studies their stories alongside the narrations concerning Imam-e-Zamana (ATFS) — carefully distinguishing established reports from interpretation.

SELECTED PROPHET

Adam (AS)

Prophet Adam (AS) is described in the Qur'an as the first human and a recipient of divine teaching. His story introduces humanity's responsibility, repentance and relationship with Allah.

Qur'anic starting points: Surah al-Baqarah 2:30–39; Surah Sad 38:71–75.

Research status: The profile introduces the Prophet's story. A specific resemblance to Imam-e-Zamana (ATFS) will be added only after its narration, source, edition and page have been verified.
