Reading the sources carefully

Hayat ul Quloob is a key source for studying the biographies and missions of the Prophets (AS). Exact volume, page and edition references should be added after checking the edition used for this research.

Any reported resemblance to Imam-e-Zamana (ATFS) will be studied separately against the relevant passages in Mikyal ul Makaram. A resemblance will not be presented as established unless the narration and its source have been verified.

Research standard
